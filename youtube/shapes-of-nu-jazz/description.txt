It's past midnight and the club is half empty. A warm Hammond organ starts to breathe, the drums lock into a lazy, irresistible groove, and a lonely trumpet cuts through the smoke with a melody you'll keep humming tomorrow. Shapes of Nu Jazz moves between jazz funk, soul jazz and modern grooves: music for night drives, creative work, a glass of something good or a slow conversation that doesn't want to end. 🎺🌙✨

Full album: "Shapes of Nu Jazz" (Hip Code Records)

About

Jazz from London to Verona.

Hip Code Records is an independent jazz label. We follow every record from the studio to the release: masters, credits, codes, worldwide digital distribution and the protection of writers' and musicians' rights.

Publishing for the catalogue is handled by Moveubabe Music Publishing Group. Hip Code Records is part of the Moveubabe Music group.
